# Real model outputs, committed verbatim

Not hand-written approximations of a defect — actual `us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0` responses captured during
Phase 5 Stage 8 and Phase 4's closing verification, against the real prompts in `PROMPT-REGISTRY.md` and
the real claim `CLM-2608-00042-4` (12 rental days used, 8 remaining, $400 left).

They exist so the Stage 4 detectors can be proven to have teeth against output a real model really
produced, rather than against an example written by the same person who wrote the detector — which would
make the detector green by construction and prove nothing.

`rental_clean_20260811.txt` is as important as the two bad ones. A detector that flags every answer is
useless, and without a real negative there is nothing to establish that it does not.
